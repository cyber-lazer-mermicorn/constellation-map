# Quality standard

## Code law
- First pass is last pass
- No placeholders
- Born to run, then self-heal where designed
- Proprietary by default

## Evidence law
- Claim only what is proven
- Exact blockers > false success
- `tested` | `toolchain_gated` | `service_gated` | `hardware_gated` | `formally_verified`

## Delivery law
- Recruiter/operator can answer in under two minutes: what, where, when, why, how
- Dual exhibits (easy + advanced) when teaching a boundary
- Receipts for missions that claim success

## Review checklist
1. Unique boundary owned?
2. Runnable or exact-blocker honest?
3. No bloat / no theater?
4. Rights posture correct?
5. Linked from constellation-map nodes/edges?
